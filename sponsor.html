Why Sponsor Smart-Rename?

Smart-Rename: Enterprise Edition (v2.1.0) is a powerful Python script designed for enterprise workflows, offering:

  • Batch renaming with `.gitignore` support.
  • Non-ASCII text handling, including Persian.
  • Parallel processing for large-scale operations.
  • YAML configuration for complex workflows.

Your contributions fund feature development, security updates, documentation, and enterprise support via Tidelift.
